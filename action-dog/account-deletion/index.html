Last updated: 2026-03-23

This page explains how a user can request deletion of an Action Dog account and related data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to silverkies@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line [Action Dog Account Deletion Request].
  3. Include the Google account email used to sign in to Action Dog.
We may ask for additional information required to verify account ownership before deletion is processed.

What will be deleted

  • Action Dog account linkage information used for sign-in
  • Leaderboard/ranking records associated with the account
  • Profile data stored for Action Dog service operation

What may be retained

  • Information that must be retained to comply with applicable laws
  • Operational records that must be retained for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons, only for the required retention period

Processing time

Deletion requests are normally processed within 7 business days after identity verification is complete.

Delete data without deleting the account

Action Dog does not currently provide a separate in-app flow to delete only part of the account data. If you need ranking-related data removed, contact the email above and describe the request.
